Goes and looks. Leaves nothing behind.

A quiet research tool

Celina looks things up without an account, so what comes back is shaped by the topic instead of your history. Everything stays on your machine.

Download for Windows Free. No account. Requires the Edge WebView2 runtime (standard on Windows 11).

What she does

One search box, fanning out across sources that need no login and no key, blended into one list you can actually read.

  • ResearchOpen-access scholarly sources — OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Crossref.
  • WebA resilient search chain, read privately.
  • RecentFresh news, via Google News.
  • ContextWikipedia, full-text.

A grounded answer cites across the blend, checked against what was actually read before it's shown to you. Read any source privately, keep what matters as a clean research note. No account, no profile — the visit isn't tied to you.

The claim, precisely

No account. No cookies. No history shaping what comes back.

Celina doesn't log in as you. Sites see a visitor with no history, so results aren't personalized and the visit isn't tied to your accounts.

Your IP address is not hidden. If you need that, run a VPN alongside her.

Bring your own model

Five backends, behind one gateway. Not tied to any single vendor — switch anytime in Settings. Keys stay in a file on this machine; there's no account, so there's nothing for us to hand over.
